Coursework

Coursework in DATA 23700 falls into four categories:

  • Assignments: four assessments of core course concepts, each involving analyzing and visualizing a specific dataset.
  • Project: a group project with four deliverables (Dataset Audit, Written Proposal, oral Project Defense, and Project Repository), serving as the final in DATA 23700. See the specification documents in the project/ folder for details.
  • Exercises: in-class activities intended as low-stakes practice with course concepts and tools. Exercises begin during class and may be completed at home.
  • Quizzes: short pop quizzes given in class that serve as an attendance mechanism.

Grading criteria for each category are described in the Evaluation section below.

Evaluation

We use a form of grading known as specifications grading in this course. The goal of specifications grading is to help students focus on their mastery of the material and identify areas for improvement as the quarter progresses. Scores are intentionally de-emphasized in favor of feedback.

Final grades will be determined based on participation, assignments, and the project.

Assignments and the project

Assignments and the project will be evaluated using an S/N/U scale:

  • Satisfactory (S): The student demonstrates sufficient mastery of the material, with substantive engagement with the concepts. Complete submissions that follow directions but show no engagement with the underlying ideas do not earn S.
  • Needs Improvement (N): The student has put in a good-faith effort but has not yet reached mastery. This includes rote or superficial work that fulfills the prompt, as well as work with meaningful conceptual gaps or errors that could be addressed via feedback.
  • Ungradable (U): The student did not submit, did not follow directions, or completed less than half of the work.

When reviewing assignments, we evaluate both:

  • Quality of visualizations including but not limited to design choices such as encodings, transformations, and scales.
  • Quality of write-up including but not limited to clear communication, logical arguments, and cogent rationales for design and analysis choices.

The project consists of four deliverables (Dataset Audit, Written Proposal, oral Project Defense, and Project Repository). Each deliverable is evaluated on multiple criteria, described in its specification document in the project/ folder.

The specifications for each assignment and each project deliverable include more precise descriptions of what is expected for a score of Satisfactory.

There are a total of 8 S/N/U scores for assignments (two per assignment across four assignments) and 13 S/N/U scores for the project (spread across the four project deliverables), for a total of 21 S/N/U scores.

Exercises and Quizes

Exercises and quizes evaluate whether a student is keeping up with the course. They are scored with participation points, which add up over the quarter to determine what grade a student is eligible for (see Final grades). Exercises are intended as low-stakes opportunities to practice, and students should attempt all of them. Quizes are indended to incentivize attendance.

Exercises provide the majority of the participation points that a student can earn in DATA 23700. Exercises will be graded on completeness and following directions. Exercises must be turned in on time to receive credit. No partial credit will be awarded for exercises.

Quizes provide additional opportunities to earn participation points. Quizes are graded based on whether a student was present in class and submits them. Students will not be allowed to make up participation points for missed quizes.

There are a total of 7 participation points for exercises and 6 participation points for quizes, for 13 total. Each exercise and each quiz is worth one participation point.
